## Phone banner prompt

Create one photorealistic banner photograph for a small mobile phone and electronics shop website. Wide landscape, approximately 16:9 composition. Commercial studio product photography on a very light neutral grey seamless background and tabletop, soft natural shadows, restrained elegant styling. Arrange two generic modern smartphones, one showing a dark luminous purple abstract display and one showing its graphite rear cameras, on the right half of the image. Add one small pair of black wireless earbuds in their open charging case near the far right lower corner. Devices should look realistic, with believable proportions and materials; no logos, brands, labels or text. Leave the left 45 percent entirely uncluttered, light grey and empty, for HTML headings and buttons to be added separately. No illustration, no cartoon style, no floating decorations, no people, no lettering, no watermark. Main subject fairly large but with generous margins for responsive cropping. This is generic illustrative marketing imagery, not a photograph of a specific product model.

## Accessories banner prompt

Photorealistic commercial product photograph for an electronics shop promotion tile, wide landscape about 16:9. Neutral very light warm grey seamless studio tabletop. On the RIGHT HALF arrange a realistic pair of matte black wireless over-ear headphones, an open black earbuds charging case, and a small white USB charger with a neatly coiled cable. No brands, no logos, no readable text, no graphic overlays, no people. Leave the left 45 percent clean and nearly empty light grey for an HTML heading. Restrained natural lighting and soft realistic shadows, premium but practical electronics retail photography. No cartoon style, no illustration, no colourful gradients. Devices are generic illustrative marketing props, not specific inventory models.

## Sample phone prompt

Create a photorealistic studio product image used as an explicitly illustrative placeholder for an electronics catalogue. Two generic modern smartphones, one front view and one rear view, standing side by side with a slight tasteful angle. Graphite dark grey metal rear with believable small camera lenses, front showing a restrained purple abstract screen. No logos or identifiable brand, no letters, no text. No accessories, no charger, no packaging. Almost white light grey seamless background and tabletop, subtle soft shadow. Devices centred and large, with comfortable space on all sides, square composition suitable for a product card and responsive crop. Commercial ecommerce packshot photograph, realistic proportions and materials. This depicts no specific phone model and is not actual shop stock. No illustrated or cartoon appearance.
